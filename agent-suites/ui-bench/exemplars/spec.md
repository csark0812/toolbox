# Reference look

The screenshots in this folder are pages the reviewer preferred: compact, simple, clearly separated. Match their density and restraint, not their content or layout.

- Page: content column up to 72rem, centred; 16px side padding on phones, 24–32px on desktop.
- Spacing: 4–8px inside an item, 12–16px between items, 24–32px between sections.
- Separation: a hairline or one spacing step between list items; at most one level of bordered container.
- Rows: list and table rows 40–56px tall; card padding at most 16px.
- Buttons: the one primary action 40px tall and solid; every other action 32px or a text link.
- Type: body 14–16px from the type scale; headings one or two steps larger, never hero-size in an app.
- Summaries: counts and totals in one inline row, not tall stat cards.
