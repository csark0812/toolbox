FAQ

Questions

Does Tallyho move money?

No. It tells you who should pay whom; you pay each other however you like.

What if someone moves out mid-month?

Settle them up early from their profile; their balance is closed and the rest carries on.

Can we split unevenly?

Yes. Any expense can be split by share, by amount or by room size.
