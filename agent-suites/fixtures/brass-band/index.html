Rehearsals

The senior band rehearses on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 19:30 to 21:30, at St Peter's Hall, Mill Road, Ashworth. Visitors are welcome to sit in on any rehearsal.

Learner band

Saturdays 10:00 to 11:30 at St Peter's Hall. All ages, complete beginners welcome. Instruments are loaned free of charge.

Join us

Subs are £15 a month (under-18s free). Current vacancies: 2nd cornet, tenor horn, Eb bass. Contact our secretary Jan Whitworth on 01632 960 391 or secretary@ashworthbrass.example.

Book the band

We play weddings, fêtes, remembrance services and funerals across the Vale. Please ask our secretary for a quote; we need at least eight weeks' notice.
