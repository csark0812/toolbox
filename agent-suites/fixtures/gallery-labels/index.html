Spring hang

Twelve works on the theme of the river, hung in the long room until the end of May.

  • Low Water at Ferry Steps

    Agnes Morrow (1891-1960)

    Oil on board, 1934

    Painted from the gallery's own back door at the lowest tide of the year.

  • The Lock Keeper's Daughter

    Tobias Wren (b. 1978)

    Graphite and watercolour, 2019

    One of a series of portraits of people who work on the canal.

    On loan from the Wren family

  • Barges, Evening

    Unknown artist

    Linocut, about 1950

    Found in the archive in 2021; the printer's mark is unread.

  • Flood Line

    Priya Natarajan (b. 1985)

    Cyanotype on cotton, 2024

    Exposed on the riverbank over a single afternoon.

    On loan from the artist
