Prizes

Pots are presented on the clubhouse balcony half an hour after the last final.

  • Ferry Cup: open coxed four
  • Towpath Sculls: men's J16 single
  • Lockkeeper's Bowl: women's masters double
  • Osier Plate: women's J18 quad
  • Anderson Trophy: men's novice eight
  • Meadow Salver: mixed double
  • Kingfisher Pots: women's open pair
  • Veterans' Tankard: men's masters four
  • Hollingford Challenge Cup: club with the most wins
