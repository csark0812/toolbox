# Follow-ups from Saturday's tournament

1. With six players the byes went to seeds 5 and 6 instead of seeds 1 and 2, so our two best players had to play an extra match. Byes should go to the top seeds.
2. The scoreboard app wants the draw as CSV. Add `--format csv` to `npm run draw`, printing a header `match,home,away` and one row per match, with `BYE` for an empty slot.
