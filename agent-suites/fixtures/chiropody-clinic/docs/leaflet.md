# Looking after your feet

Fernside Foot Clinic offers routine foot care, nail care and advice on footwear.

**Booking.** Ring us on (01632) 960123, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. If you need to cancel, please give us a day's notice.

**Nail surgery.** We refer you to the podiatry department at Westmoor Hospital, 01632 960999.

**Out of hours.** For urgent problems when we are closed, ring NHS 111.
