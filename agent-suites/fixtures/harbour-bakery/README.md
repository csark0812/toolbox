# Harbour Street Bakery website

Brief from the owner, Ines Duarte:

> We're a small sourdough bakery at 14 Harbour Street, Peniche, on the Portuguese coast. We bake once a
> day, starting at 4:30, and when the counter is empty we close, usually around 13:00. Open Wednesday
> to Sunday. Our loaves: the harbour loaf (country sourdough, €4.80), a seaweed and rye loaf (€5.20, the
> seaweed comes from the boats down the road), and a fennel and raisin loaf on Saturdays only (€5.50).
> Pastries: custard tarts (€1.40), cardamom buns (€2.20). You can reserve loaves by text before 20:00
> the day before: +351 912 000 414. We have no logo, no photos and no colours picked yet.

The site is plain static HTML, CSS and JS in this folder; `index.html` is the page.
