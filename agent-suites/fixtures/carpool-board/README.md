# Carpool board

Lift sharing for staff at the Kestrel Park business estate. Drivers register their usual route into work (as points on the estate's map grid, in kilometres) and the weekdays they drive; riders post requests with a pickup point and a date.

Privacy: riders only ever see a driver's first name and how far out of their way the pickup is. A driver's phone number and home street are shared with a rider only after the driver accepts the request.

`npm run drivers` lists the registered drivers. `npm test` runs the tests.
