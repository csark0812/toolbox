Work in a Victorian ropewalk

Desks and studios in a 300-metre brick hall on Rope Lane, Faversham.

Book a visit

The hall

Rope was laid here for the navy from 1790 to 1961. Today there are 64 desks down both sides of the hall, six glass studios at the far end, two meeting rooms in the old tarring house and a kitchen where the hemp store used to be.
