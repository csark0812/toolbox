# House rules

Pinned up by the door and sent to every band when they first book.

- We're open 10:00 to 23:00, seven days a week, apart from the days on the board (Christmas and Boxing Day, and anything else we have to close for).
- Rooms are charged by the minute. Daytime rate until 18:00, evening rate from 18:00, so a session that runs across six o'clock pays each rate for its own part.
- Amp hire is £4 a session on top of the room.
- Cancel at least 48 hours before your session starts and you pay nothing. Cancel with less than 48 hours' notice and you pay for the room in full.
- Leave the room as you found it. Drum kits stay in the room.
