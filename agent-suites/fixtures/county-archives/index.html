Before you come

You need a reader's ticket. It is free: bring photo ID and proof of address dated within the last 3 months.

Order documents online at least 3 working days before your visit. You can order up to 6 items per visit.

In the reading room

  • Pencils only.
  • No bags: lockers take a £1 coin, which you get back.
  • Photography is allowed with a £5 day permit. No flash.

Copies

Scans £3.50 per image. Certified copies £12 each.
