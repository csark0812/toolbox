# Hartley & Daughter Typewriters

Brief from Ruth Hartley for our first proper website. One page is fine.

## Who we are

My dad opened the workshop in 1979 and I took it over in 2015. We repair and restore **manual typewriters only**: no electrics, no word processors. Everything is done by hand at the bench in the back of the shop, and every machine leaves with a typed test sheet showing every key.
