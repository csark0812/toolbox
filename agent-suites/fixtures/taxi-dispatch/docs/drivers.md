# Brookside Taxis drivers' handbook

Welcome to Brookside Taxis. A few things every driver needs to know.

- Pick up your day sheet from the office, or print it with `npm run sheet -- <date>`.
- Between jobs, wait on the Brookside Taxi Rank on Station Road. The rank belongs to the council and is shared with the other firms, so keep to the marked bays.
- Start the meter when the customer gets in, not when you arrive. Waiting time is charged by the minute.
- Account customers do not pay in the car. Ask them to sign the job slip.
- Wear your Brookside Taxis badge at all times.
