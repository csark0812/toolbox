# Market Day

The market opens at six, but the good tomatoes are gone by seven. Marta told us this on our first evening, and we didn't believe her. On the second morning we went at half past seven and found only crates and a dog asleep beneath a table.

She sells honey from her brother's hives in 1987 jars she has collected over the years, none of them matching. Each one has a paper label written by hand. When we asked which was best she shrugged and said it depends on the bees.
