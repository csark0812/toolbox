Flower and Produce Show

Saturday 14 August, St Mary's Hall, 2pm

Show classes

  • Class 1

    Three dahlias, decorative, one variety

    Shown in a vase, stems at least 30cm.

  • Class 2

    Five sweet peas, mixed colours

    Any foliage of their own.

  • Class 3

    A vase of garden flowers

    At least five kinds, arranged by the exhibitor.

  • Class 4

    Six runner beans

    Matched for length, tops left on.

  • Class 5

    Three onions, dressed

    Shown on a ring, tops tied.

  • Class 6

    The heaviest marrow

    Weighed on the day by the steward.

  • Class 7

    A Victoria sponge

    Made to the schedule recipe, jam filling only.

  • Class 8

    A jar of blackcurrant jam

    Plain lid, labelled with the month made.

  • Class 9

    A photograph: 'Walton in bloom'

    Unmounted print, up to 7 by 5 inches.

Entering

Entries are 50p a class and close at 8pm on the Thursday before the show. Bring your exhibits to the hall between 8am and 10.30am on show day.

Enter online https://forms.waltonflowershow.org.uk/entries/2027
