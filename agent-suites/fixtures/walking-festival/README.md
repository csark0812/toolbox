# Wolds Walking Festival 2027

Brief from the festival committee. We need a single page that sells the festival and gets people to book.

## The festival

- Dates: Friday 14 to Monday 17 May 2027
- Base: Market Weighton Town Hall, where every walk starts or has a free minibus to its start
- 22 guided walks over four days, led by volunteer leaders who know the chalk valleys
- Booking opens 1 February 2027. Walks are capped at 20 people, so popular ones fill.

## Tickets

| Ticket        | Price | Includes                               |
| ------------- | ----- | -------------------------------------- |
| Day ticket    | £12   | Any walks on one day, plus the minibus |
| Festival pass | £38   | All four days, plus Saturday's ceilidh |
| Under-16s     | Free  | With a paying adult                    |

## Three walks to feature

| Walk               | Distance | Grade    | Day      |
| ------------------ | -------- | -------- | -------- |
| Thixendale Dales   | 11 miles | Hard     | Saturday |
| Millington Wood    | 5 miles  | Moderate | Friday   |
| Goodmanham Springs | 3 miles  | Easy     | Monday   |

## Practical

- Dogs welcome on lead on all walks except Millington Wood (grazing sheep).
