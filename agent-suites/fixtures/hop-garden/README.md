# Hop garden rota

The picking weekend rota for Tanyard Hop Garden. A static page: open `index.html` in a browser. `app.js` handles the Print button and the day filter. Styles are in `styles.css`. The markup is generated by the rota spreadsheet's export, so changes go in `styles.css`.

## On screen

The header stays at the top of the window, the filters sit in a column on the left, and on a phone the rota tables scroll sideways inside their section. The committee is happy with how it looks on screen.

## Printed

The rota is printed on A4 portrait and pinned up in the stringing shed. The barn printer is set to save ink and never prints background colours, so the printed sheet has to work in black text on white paper.

- Only the rota prints: the farm name and weekend dates, the heading and intro, both days, the "On the day" notes and the footer. The menu, the filters, the Print button and the "Back to top" link stay off the paper.
- The farm name and dates print once, at the top of the first page, and nothing sits over the rota on later pages.
- Every column of both tables fits across the page. Nothing is cut off at the right, squeezed into a narrow strip, or hidden in a scroll box, and the page isn't scaled down: printed text stays at least 12px.
- Every bit of printed text is readable without its background colour: at least 4.5:1 against white paper. Status words ("Full", "Needs 2", "Cancelled") still print, and cancelled shifts are still readable.
- A shift's row is never split across two pages, and a day's heading never sits on its own at the foot of a page.
- Every link that prints shows its address after its text, so people can type it in or send an email.

`npm test` checks the stylesheet parses and the markup has every shift.
