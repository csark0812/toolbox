On the day

Park in the oast yard and sign in at the stringing shed. Tea and bacon rolls from 7am. If you can fill a gap, put your name down on the sign-up sheet.

Children are welcome with an adult but can't use the bine knives.

Questions about your shift? Email the rota team or ring Marian on 01622 555 018.
