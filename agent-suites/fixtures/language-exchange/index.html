// LANGUAGE EXCHANGE

Speak a new language with real people

Every Tuesday, learners and native speakers meet at shared tables, swap languages and make friends. No apps, no textbooks, just conversation.

Join the next session →

// HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps

🪑

Pick a table

Sit at the table for the language you are learning. Each one has a volunteer host.

🔄

Swap every 20 minutes

Half the time in your language, half in theirs. A bell tells everyone when to switch.

🔁

Come back next week

Same table, same faces. Most regulars find a partner within a month.

// FAQ

Questions

Do I need to be fluent?

No. Every table has beginners, and the hosts keep the conversation moving.

What if my language isn't listed?

Tell us at the door. We open a new table once three people ask for it.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. Everyone pays the same £4, whether they are learning or helping.
