# Critique

<!-- source-of-truth: taste screenshot critique passes and the written critique format. -->
<!-- doc-meta: owner=eng | last-reviewed=2026-10-21 -->

Judge only the screenshots. If a problem is visible in a picture it is real, whatever the code says. Look at every shot at every width, top to bottom, before writing anything.

## Passes

1. **Five-second test.** From the first screen alone, can a stranger say what this is, for whom, and what to do? Is the primary action visible without scrolling at 390px?
2. **Idea.** Does the page belong to this subject, or could a competitor's name be swapped in? Does the idea survive past the first screen?
3. **Hierarchy.** One dominant thing per screen. Are sizes clearly different, or is everything medium?
4. **Type.** Did the intended fonts render? Headlines within two lines? Comfortable line length? Body large enough?
5. **Colour.** Does the palette come from the subject or the brand? Is the accent rare? Is any text hard to read?
6. **Layout.** Equal margins? Anything huddled to one side? Empty stretches? Do neighbouring sections differ?
7. **Content.** Is everything the brief asked for present, complete and findable: prices, hours, routes, answers? Anything invented that should not be?
8. **Phone.** Is the 390px page composed for a phone, or a squeezed desktop? Tap targets, wrapping, tables, navigation.
9. **Tells.** Any item from the generic-tells table in [direction](direction.md)?
10. **Finish.** Broken images, missing fonts, overlaps, clipped text, stray scrollbars, unstyled states.

## Written critique

Write it down each round, in this shape:

```text
Round N, widths 390/768/1440
Five-second read: <what a stranger would say>
Score (1-10): idea _, hierarchy _, type _, colour _, layout _, content _, phone _
Top three problems, biggest first:
1. <problem> at <width, screen> -> <fix>
2. ...
3. ...
```

Fix the top three, rerun the camera, look again. Stop after three rounds or when no problem scores the page below 7, and report the last round's weakest score honestly.
