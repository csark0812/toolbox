# Crystallize research basis

**Source of truth for** evidence and limits behind Socratic crystallization.

<!-- doc-meta: owner=eng | last-reviewed=2026-07-29 -->

Read when calibrating reframing gates or making a research claim. Not for every dialogue turn.

## Evidence posture

- Problem finding can improve outcomes when the ask is fuzzy — not when the spec is already settled.
- Alternate frames reduce crystallizing the first fluent wording.
- Crystallization is not solution design — stay in problem space unless the user asks otherwise.

## Problem finding and reframing

Naming one alternate problem frame before crystallizing exposes whether the initial prompt hardens too early.

**Confidence:** Moderate to high for ill-defined creative/strategic tasks; low for rote or fully specified work.

**Does not transfer:** Mandatory reframing on every ticket; Socratic multi-agent debate (different mechanism).

- Abdulla, A. M., et al. (2020). Problem finding and creativity: A meta-analytic review. _Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts._
- Getzels, J. W. (1975). Problem-Finding and the Inventiveness of Solutions. _Journal of Creative Behavior._

## Socratic dialogue limits

Human–agent Socratic elicitation is not the same as agent–agent Socratic debate papers; do not claim hallucination-mitigation effects from dialogue alone.

**Confidence:** Low for direct transfer from MAD/Socratic-elenchus LLM papers to this skill.
