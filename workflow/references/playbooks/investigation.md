# Investigation

<!-- source-of-truth: workflow playbook for a read-only question about how or why something works -->
<!-- doc-meta: owner=eng | last-reviewed=2026-10-15 -->

**You own** the answer and its evidence. Change nothing.

1. Restate the question as one checkable claim.
2. Settle it with probe against primary evidence.
3. Push the load-bearing fact down the evidence ladder as far as is cheap.

**Reply contract:** verdict first, the evidence ladder level reached, citations, and what remains unknown.
