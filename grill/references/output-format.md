# Grill output

<!-- doc-meta: owner=eng | last-reviewed=2026-09-01 -->

Use only the sections that help the next action. Write in pragmatic Simple English.

```markdown
## What we agreed

- [Outcome or decision and its reason]

## What would reopen this

- [Evidence or changed condition that defeats a consequential choice]

## Still open

- [Deferred or unresolved material question]

## Next step

- [Smallest safe action, or the process that should take over]
```

For a narrow choice, one short paragraph can replace the sections. Do not repeat the full dialogue, emit empty headings, or invent a completeness checklist.
