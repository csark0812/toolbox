# Strict english

<!-- source-of-truth: Rewrite text in constrained plain English when explicitly requested, preserving meaning and uncertainty -->
<!-- doc-meta: owner=eng | last-reviewed=2026-09-30 -->

Explicit invocation only. Use familiar words, direct subjects and verbs, short useful sentences and consistent technical terms. Keep necessary names, code and quoted errors unchanged. Preserve hedges and uncertainty; do not turn may into will merely to simplify. Identify unavoidable specialized terms briefly. This is practical constrained-English writing, not certified ASD-STE100 compliance. Return the rewrite and only meaningful ambiguities or meaning changes. Ordinary engineering/chat writing uses the public natural-language standard.
